What they are. The Essential, Total, and VIP plans are recurring subscription memberships (billed monthly or annually) for one registered pet. Each plan includes the services listed on its card plus a discount on additional services. Included services are granted per period (e.g., 1 bath per month) and do not roll over to the next period unless otherwise stated.

Opening pricing. All currently published membership prices are limited-time opening promotional prices and vary by pet size (Small, Medium, Large, XL). BrightPuppy may change prices in the future; any change will be communicated in advance and will not affect a period already paid.

Recurring billing and automatic renewal. By subscribing you authorize BrightPuppy (via Stripe) to save your payment method and automatically charge it at the start of each period (monthly or annual) at the then-current price, until you cancel under this policy. We will send a receipt for each charge and a reminder before renewal. You may request cancellation at any time by contacting us (see Contact) or from your account; cancellation takes effect at the end of the current period and is subject to the minimum commitment below.

Minimum commitment. Memberships have a 3-month minimum commitment (or the chosen annual term). If you cancel before completing the minimum commitment, an early-cancellation adjustment will apply, equal to the difference between the member price and the regular (à-la-carte) price of the services you already received during the membership; alternatively, BrightPuppy may charge a one (1) month penalty. This prevents misuse of member pricing for a single service.

Re-enrollment. Opening promotional prices apply once per client/pet. If you cancel and later re-subscribe, the current regular price and a reactivation fee may apply.

Additional services & sensitive skin. Services not included in your plan (e.g., dental cleaning, special treatments) are billed separately, with your plan discount where applicable, and may be paid and scheduled for the date you choose. Use of sensitive-skin / hypoallergenic products is an optional paid add-on (flat fee, size-independent).

Tips. Tips are voluntary and go to the grooming team. You may leave an optional tip after each visit.

Rescheduling & no-shows. Member appointments follow the general cancellation/no-show policy on the Grooming page. Credits unused due to repeated no-shows may be forfeited.
