1. Puppy/kitten reservation deposit

A deposit (typically 30%) reserves your pet and removes it from availability. Under regular conditions, if the buyer chooses to cancel, the deposit is non-refundable. The remaining balance is due on the delivery date, unless you choose to pay in advance. These conditions are detailed in the signed sales contract.

2. When the cause is ours (refund / replacement)

Sales are not final when the responsibility is BrightPuppy's:

  • If for reasons of ours we cannot source the agreed puppy, we refund your deposit in full.
  • If the puppy presents a health condition due to our cause and must be returned to us, we replace it with another of equal value, or refund your deposit if you prefer not to wait.

3. Payment plans (layaway)

Payment-plan installments are 100% refundable if you decide to withdraw before receiving your puppy. You may pay the balance at any time to bring delivery forward.

4. Grooming

  • Cancel with more than 24 hours notice: no charge.
  • Cancel with less than 24 hours notice: 50% of the service.
  • No-show: 100% charge.
  • Rescheduling with at least 6 hours notice is free.

5. Health guarantee

All our puppies and kittens include a written health guarantee. See coverage and timeframes on the Guarantee page.

6. How to request a refund

Message us on WhatsApp at +1 (808) 492-8294 or by email, with your name and purchase/appointment details. We will review your case under this policy.

7. Processing

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method (via Stripe) and may take several business days to appear, depending on your bank.

8. Chargebacks

If you have any issue with a charge, contact us first: we resolve the vast majority of cases directly and faster than a chargeback.
