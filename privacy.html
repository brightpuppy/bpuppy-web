1. Information we collect

  • Data you give us: name, email, phone, address/ZIP code and the details of your request (breed of interest, preferences, grooming pet).
  • Payment data: processed securely by Stripe (and integrated instruments like Klarna, Affirm or Cash App). We do not store your card numbers on our servers.
  • Content you upload: reviews, photos, messages and posts you share on B Social, in review forms, or when you send us images (see section 8).
  • Usage & analytics data: when you accept analytics cookies, for each visit we record your IP address, approximate location (country, region and city derived from the IP), internet provider (ISP), device type, operating system and browser, language, landing page and referrer. We use this to understand traffic, improve the site and protect the security of the business. If you choose essential cookies only, we do not collect this analytics.

2. How we use your information

  • To handle your requests, reservations, payment plans and grooming appointments.
  • To communicate with you by email, SMS and WhatsApp (confirmations, reminders, receipts and service notices), including automated messages.
  • To process payments securely and prevent fraud.
  • To improve our site and services and measure our advertising.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. SMS & WhatsApp messaging (how to opt out)

When you give us your number and agree to the consent notice on our forms, you authorize BrightPuppy to contact you by SMS and WhatsApp, including automated messages, about your request, your appointments and updates. Consent to marketing messages is not a condition of purchase. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply from your carrier.

How to opt out: reply STOP to any SMS to stop receiving texts; reply HELP for help. On WhatsApp you can message us "STOP" or block the number. You can also unsubscribe from emails using the unsubscribe link at the bottom of each message, or by emailing hello@bpuppy.us. Even if you opt out of marketing messages, we may still send you necessary transactional messages (e.g., a purchase or appointment confirmation).

4. Third-party advertising & analytics

With your cookie consent we use third-party tools to measure traffic and advertising:

  • Google Analytics — site usage and traffic measurement.
  • Google Ads — measurement of our ad conversions and remarketing. To measure accurately we may share contact data (email and/or phone) with Google in hashed (encrypted) form; Google uses it only to match the conversion and never sees it in readable text (Enhanced Conversions).
  • Meta Pixel (Facebook/Instagram) — conversion measurement and audiences for our ads on Meta platforms.

These tools may use cookies and identifiers. You can disable them by choosing essential cookies only in our notice, and also via Google and Meta ad settings.

5. Cookies & analytics

When you enter the site you'll see a cookie notice with two options: Accept (all) or Configure (choose essential only, or also enable analytics). Essential cookies keep the site working; analytics and advertising cookies let us measure visits and conversions and, in that case, we record the data described in section 1 (including IP address and approximate location) and enable the tools in section 4. We only enable them after you accept. You can change your choice by clearing your browser data, and also control cookies from your browser settings. We honor the Global Privacy Control (GPC) browser signal as an opt-out request (see section 11).

6. Who we share it with (subprocessors)

We share only what is necessary with providers that help us operate, under their own privacy policies:

  • Stripe — payment processing (includes Klarna, Affirm, Cash App).
  • Supabase — secure database and storage.
  • Cloudflare — hosting, content delivery and site security.
  • Resend — transactional and marketing email delivery.
  • Google (Workspace, Analytics, Ads) — email, metrics and advertising conversion measurement.
  • Meta (Facebook/Instagram) — advertising measurement (Meta Pixel) and WhatsApp messaging.
  • Behold.so — displaying our public Instagram feed on the site.
  • fal.ai — image/AI processing for site features.
  • SMS/WhatsApp provider — our messaging platform (WhatsApp Business via Meta) used to send texts and WhatsApp messages. We will list the SMS provider's name here once that channel is enabled.

We may also disclose information where required by law or to protect our rights.

7. Security & retention periods

We apply reasonable measures to protect your information. We keep each type of data only as long as needed:

  • Leads and clients (name, contact, requests, purchase and grooming history): while the relationship is active and for up to 7 years after your last interaction or transaction, to meet tax, accounting and contract obligations.
  • Payment records and contracts: up to 7 years per U.S. tax and accounting rules.
  • Web visit analytics (web_visits): maximum 1 year.
  • Marketing consent and opt-out records: until you withdraw consent and, afterward, the minimum time needed to evidence your opt-out.
  • Uploaded content (reviews, photos, posts): while published or until you request removal (see section 8).

8. Content and photos you upload (media release)

If you post a review, take part in B Social, or send us photos of your pet or the delivery, that content may appear on our site and social channels. By submitting it, you represent that it is yours or that you have permission to share it, and you grant us a non-exclusive license to display it for BrightPuppy's service and promotional purposes. Do not post sensitive information about other people without their permission. You may ask us to remove your content at any time by emailing hello@bpuppy.us. Formal image/likeness authorization is set out in our media release document.

9. Your rights

You may request to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal information, or opt out of marketing communications. Contact us and we will handle your request under applicable law. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

10. Minors

Our services and sales are intended for individuals 18 and older, and the site is not directed to children under 13 for data collection. Our kids' game ("Quiz") collects no personal information: it only uses a nickname or first name to personalize the on-device experience, with no email, phone or address, and we do not tie it to an identity. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child gave us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. California privacy (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know, access, correct and delete your personal information, and not to be discriminated against for exercising those rights.

We do not sell or share your personal information for money in the traditional sense. Our use of advertising tools (Google Ads and Meta Pixel) may be considered a "sale" or "sharing" under California law. You can exercise your right to "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" by choosing essential cookies only in our notice or by emailing hello@bpuppy.us. We also honor the Global Privacy Control (GPC) browser signal and treat it as a valid opt-out request.

12. International transfers

We serve clients in the USA and other countries. Your information may be processed on servers located in the USA.

13. Changes

We may update this policy; the current version is always published here with its date.

14. Contact for privacy requests
