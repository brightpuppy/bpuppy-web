Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your information when you use bpuppy.us. Your trust comes first.

1. Information we collect

  • Data you give us: name, email, phone, address/ZIP code and the details of your request (breed of interest, preferences, grooming pet).
  • Payment data: processed securely by Stripe (and integrated instruments like Klarna, Affirm or Cash App). We do not store your card numbers on our servers.
  • Usage & analytics data: when you accept analytics cookies, for each visit we record your IP address, approximate location (country, region and city derived from the IP), internet provider (ISP), device type, operating system and browser, language, landing page and referrer. We use this to understand traffic, improve the site and protect the security of the business. If you choose essential cookies only, we do not collect this analytics.

2. How we use your information

  • To handle your requests, reservations, payment plans and grooming appointments.
  • To communicate with you (confirmations, reminders, receipts and service notices).
  • To process payments securely and prevent fraud.
  • To improve our site and services.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Who we share it with

We share only what is necessary with providers that help us operate, under their own privacy policies:

  • Stripe — payment processing (includes Klarna, Affirm, Cash App).
  • Supabase — secure database and storage.
  • Cloudflare — site hosting and security.
  • Resend / Google Workspace — email delivery.
  • Google Analytics — usage metrics.

We may also disclose information where required by law.

4. Cookies & analytics

When you enter the site you'll see a cookie notice with two options: Accept (all) or Configure (choose essential only, or also enable analytics). Essential cookies keep the site working; analytics cookies let us measure visits and, in that case, we record the data described in section 1 (including IP address and approximate location). We only enable analytics after you accept. You can change your choice by clearing your browser data, and also control cookies from your browser settings. We keep the detail of each visit for a maximum of 1 year.

5. Security & retention

We apply reasonable measures to protect your information. We retain data as long as needed to provide the service and to meet legal and accounting obligations.

6. Your rights

You may request to access, correct or delete your personal information, or opt out of marketing communications. Contact us and we will handle your request under applicable law (including, where applicable, California privacy law).

7. Minors

Our services are intended for individuals 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

8. International transfers

We serve clients in the USA and other countries. Your information may be processed on servers located in the USA.

9. Changes

We may update this policy; the current version is always published here with its date.
