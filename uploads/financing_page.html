Common questions
Everything you need to know — straight answers, no fine print
Will applying for financing affect my credit score?
Most "Pay in 4" options like Klarna and Afterpay use a soft credit check that does not affect your score. Longer-term loans through Affirm may use a soft pre-qualification first — only a hard pull if you accept the loan. You'll always know before you commit.
Are there any hidden fees?
No. The "Pay in 4" plans charge zero interest if you pay on time. The 12 and 24-month loans have a clear APR shown upfront. We never add a markup or financing fee on our side — what the provider charges is what you pay.
Can I pay off my loan early?
Yes, all our financing partners allow early payoff with no prepayment penalty. Pay it off whenever works for you.
What if I don't qualify for financing?
If one provider declines, we can try another — they each have different criteria. Worst case, you can split the payment with a partner or family member. Just call us and we'll figure it out together. We've helped many families find a way.
Does financing include delivery and the puppy's care kit?
Yes. Whatever total you finance covers the puppy, vaccinations, health certificate, travel-readiness, and our standard guarantees. Delivery costs are quoted separately depending on your location, and you can choose whether to add them to the financed amount.
How fast can I bring my puppy home?
Once approved, most families have their puppy home within 7–10 days. We coordinate every detail with you — health checks, paperwork, and travel — so the only thing left for you is to welcome your new family member.
